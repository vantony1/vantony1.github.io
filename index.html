I work on human-robot interaction and human-computer interaction. I take a human-centered approach to developing embodied AI systems for human well-being: I co-design systems with domain experts and end users, then study them in real-world environments.

I am advised by Chien-Ming Huang in the Intuitive Computing Laboratory at Johns Hopkins. Before Hopkins, I studied computer science at the University of Rochester with Ehsan Hoque and Zhen Bai.

Research

Theme 1

Co-designing and deploying robots with end users and domain experts

Working with older adults, families, and domain experts to understand their needs, translate them into robot form and behavior, and deploy the resulting robots in homes.

ELLA IDC ’26 On-Body Robots HRI ’25 Voice Assistants for Health CHI ’25 Exercise Robots HRI ’23
Theme 2

Generative AI for expressive, conversational robots

Using language models to generate context-aware facial expressions, handle interruptions in conversation, and co-create visual stories.

Xpress HRI ’25 ID.8 ★ ACM TiiS Highlighted Paper Interruption Handling RSS ’25 Alchemist HRI ’24
Theme 3

Open platforms and real-world deployments

Low-cost, open-source robots that are easier to reproduce, modify, and deploy, and speculative prototypes that explore plants as metaphoric inspiration for HRI.

Plant-Inspired Robots ★ ACM/IEEE HRI ’26 Best Paper Honorable Mention Lantern ★ ACM/IEEE HRI ’26 Sustainability Demonstration Recognition M RA-L ’26
